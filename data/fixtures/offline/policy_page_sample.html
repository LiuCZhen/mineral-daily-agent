Critical Minerals Strategy — update

Published 2026-01-15 · Department of Industry, Science and Resources (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE)

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE PAGE — generated for offline testing, not a real government notice.

The updated strategy maintains the existing list of critical minerals and adds guidance on downstream processing investment. Projects that include domestic refining capacity may receive streamlined assessment.

For lithium, the strategy notes that export volumes remain concentrated in spodumene concentrate, and that conversion capacity outside China remains limited.

Industry consultation on the revised guidance closes at the end of the quarter.
